The Executor's Real Estate Checklist

What to gather, who to call, and in what order — free, no email wall, built from 500+ estate transactions.

Before you do anything else

  • ☐ Locate the original will (if one exists)
  • ☐ Obtain multiple copies of the death certificate
  • ☐ Identify whether an enduring Power of Attorney or Committee order already exists, if the situation involves incapacity rather than death — see Power of Attorney or Committee of the Estate
  • ☐ Contact (or engage) an estate lawyer or notary — most of what follows depends on their guidance for your specific situation

Gathering the property's paperwork

  • ☐ Property title documents
  • ☐ Existing mortgage statements, if any
  • ☐ Recent property tax notices
  • ☐ Home insurance policy — confirm whether it needs to change for a vacant property
  • ☐ Strata documents, if the property is a condo or townhome
  • ☐ Utility account information

The legal process

  • ☐ File the application for Grant of Probate or Administration (your lawyer handles this, but confirm you understand the rough timeline — see our full timeline guide)
  • ☐ Confirm who has signing authority once the Grant is issued — see who signs what
  • ☐ Ask your lawyer specifically whether the home can be conditionally listed before the Grant is finalized

Preparing the property

  • ☐ Sort irreplaceable items (documents, photos, heirlooms) before any bulk clear-out — see clearing out an estate home
  • ☐ Get a professional walkthrough to separate must-fix repairs from cosmetic ones — see preparing an estate home for sale
  • ☐ Get a realistic market valuation, separate from any probate valuation already done — see valuing an estate home

Keeping beneficiaries informed

  • ☐ Communicate the plan and timeline to all beneficiaries before listing, not after
  • ☐ Document agreements about belongings and the sale process in writing

Before closing

  • ☐ Confirm all estate expenses (repairs, clean-out, commission) are being tracked for the final accounting — see executor compensation & estate expenses
  • ☐ Confirm with your lawyer how sale proceeds fit into the estate's distribution timeline

This checklist provides general guidance only and is not a substitute for legal advice specific to your situation. Please consult a lawyer or notary.
