About In Care Of Kelowna

Why this site exists as its own resource, separate from a typical real estate practice.

The short version: In Care Of Kelowna is a dedicated resource focused specifically on probate, Power of Attorney, and Committee-of-Estate real estate — the kind of sale where the seller isn't the homeowner, acting instead under legal authority on someone else's behalf. It's a specialized practice area within Mark Jontz & Associates at Royal LePage Kelowna, not a separate company.

Why this needed its own resource

Most real estate agents handle an estate sale occasionally — a few times a year, when it happens to come up. That's a different starting point than someone who's handled this specific kind of transaction repeatedly: understanding how court timelines interact with a real estate listing, what documentation an executor or Committee actually needs, and how to work alongside the family's lawyer or notary instead of around them. Families navigating this are often doing so during one of the harder periods of their lives, and they deserve information from someone who's done this specifically, not a generalist figuring it out as they go.

Who's behind it

In Care Of Kelowna is run by Mark Jontz, a REALTOR® with Royal LePage Kelowna who has built a significant part of his practice specifically around probate, Power of Attorney, and Committee-of-Estate transactions. See the full team page for his credentials and track record in this specific area.

What this site is — and isn't

Every guide on this site is written as general information to help families understand what's actually happening and what to expect — not as legal advice. Estate law and real estate law intersect in ways that genuinely vary by situation, and this site consistently points readers toward a lawyer or notary for anything specific to their own circumstances. The goal is to make sure nobody has to start that conversation from zero.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Every estate is different — please consult a lawyer or notary about your specific situation.
